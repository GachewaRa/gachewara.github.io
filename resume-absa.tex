\section{Selected Project}

\role{Aktaba --- Personal knowledge-management platform}{\href{https://aktaba.com}{aktaba.com}}
\rolecontext{Solo build \, · \, Live since early 2026 \, · \, 8 months idea to launch}

A polymath's notebook: ingest highlights from books, slice them into notes, tag them across domains, attach them to projects, reason through ideas on linked canvases. Designed, built, and operated end-to-end.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Backend:} FastAPI (Python) serving a REST API to a SvelteKit frontend. Stateless services, JWT auth, S3-backed document storage.
  \item \textbf{Data:} Postgres for relational records; Neo4j for the typed, weighted link graph between notes inside matters. Write-time fan-out keeps the two stores consistent.
  \item \textbf{Deployment:} AWS-hosted with regular backups and streaming replication. Approximately 600ms typical page latency from East Africa.
  \item In daily active use: 2,500+ notes across 120 tags, 50 projects, 90+ highlights, 20 documents.
\end{itemize}
